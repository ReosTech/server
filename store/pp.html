PROJECT PHOBOS

2147. Kharon Station drilled into the Martian moon Phobos and broke into a chamber that was never carved by human hands.

Nine days later the station went silent. Its last transmission was a single sentence, repeated for hours: "It has no eyes. It hunts the light."

You are a salvage technician, sent alone to recover five data relics, the crew's memory cores, and carry the truth out through the extraction gate.

The collapsed corridors are not empty. The Warden walks them. It sees your flashlight, hears you run, and it never sleeps.

Maintenance lockers still line the halls. Hide. Hold your breath. Wait for it to forget you.

YOU WERE CAUGHT

The Warden found you. Phobos keeps another soul.
